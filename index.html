FAQ

Questions, answered.

What makes HeaVit different from Yazio, HealthifyMe or Cal AI?

HeaVit calibrates to your body: risk thresholds matched to your ancestry, a growing Indian & Western food library with real portions, and AI that reads your bloodwork and family history. Whether you eat South Asian or Western food, it's clinical-grade guidance for your plate, not a one-size calorie counter.

Is HeaVit a substitute for a doctor or dietitian?

No. HeaVit provides personalised nutritional guidance for informational purposes. It doesn't diagnose, treat or cure disease. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before making changes, especially if you have a condition or take medication.

How does the plate scan work?

You snap a photo of your meal; AI vision identifies the foods, maps them to our food database, and scores the plate 0-100 across protein, fibre, glycemic load, refined carbs, sugar and oil, then suggests specific swaps to improve it.

Is my health data private?

Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, family-history stays on your device, and we never sell your data. Privacy and security are core to how HeaVit is built, following industry-standard data-protection practices.

Which diets does HeaVit support?

Vegetarian, eggetarian, vegan, Jain, non-vegetarian and pescatarian, all strictly respected in every meal plan and suggestion.
