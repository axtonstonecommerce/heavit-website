Why HeaVit

A shop built around evidence, not ads.

Next to the research

Every week HeaVit Weekly explains new health studies. Where a study relates to a category, relevant products are shown with it. Links are editorial and can’t be bought.

HeaVit Lab Tested

Products checked by an independent lab against their label earn a badge on every listing — a clear signal for shoppers who care about quality.

Shoppers with context

HeaVit users log meals, set goals and read ingredient labels in the app. They arrive at your product already knowing what they’re looking for.

Before you apply

What we need from sellers

  • A valid FSSAI licence covering the products you sell
  • GST registration and a registered business address
  • Labels that meet FSSAI labelling rules, with full ingredient lists
  • No claims that a product cures, treats or prevents a disease
  • A customer-support contact shown on every listing
  • Willingness to have products independently lab tested

Every product is reviewed by HeaVit before it goes live. Commercial terms are shared with approved brands during onboarding.
