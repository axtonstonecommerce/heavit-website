Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026 · Effective 8 November 2026

HeaVit handles health information, so this policy is written to be read rather than skimmed past. It says plainly what we collect, who else sees it, and what we do not do with it. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Who we are

HeaVit is operated by Axtonstone Commerce Private Limited, 17/30 Sainik Ngr NH9 Main, Rothak Rd, Old Barahi Mor, Bahadurgarh, Jhajjar, Haryana 124507, India. We are the data controller (or, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the data fiduciary) for the information described here.

For anything in this policy, write to support@heavit.com.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account — your email address, and your name if you provide one.
  • Profile — age, sex, height, weight, activity level, dietary preference, goals and any health conditions you choose to enter.
  • What you log — meals, portions, measurements, streaks, recipes you save.
  • Blood reports — a report you upload for analysis, and the values and explanation produced from it.
  • Photographs — plate photos and nutrition labels you choose to scan, and any photo you attach to a recipe.
  • Messages — what you write to HeaVit AI.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data — the IP address and request details that reach our API when the app calls it, used to run the service and prevent abuse.
  • Subscription status — which plan you hold and when it renews.

We do not use analytics or advertising software. HeaVit contains no third-party tracking SDK, no advertising identifier, and no cross-app tracking. We do not build advertising profiles and we do not sell data to anyone.

AI processing

HeaVit's guidance is generated by third-party AI models — Claude, from Anthropic, and Gemini, from Google. When you use an AI feature, the content of that request travels through our own server to the relevant provider so the model can answer it.

  • Your requests are not used to train Anthropic's models. Anthropic does not train on inputs or outputs sent through its API.
  • Providers retain a request only briefly, for abuse monitoring, and then delete it.
  • Photographs are not stored by us. A plate photo or nutrition label you scan is sent for analysis and is not saved to our servers or our database. Only the result is kept, against your account.
  • A blood report you upload is analysed the same way. The extracted values and the explanation are stored against your account so you can see them again; you can delete them from the app at any time.

An AI model can be wrong. HeaVit provides general nutritional information and wellness guidance only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making decisions about your health, diet, medication or treatment. HeaVit does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.

Your health data

The health information you give HeaVit — your meals, measurements, goals, blood-report values and conditions — is used to personalise your guidance and for nothing else. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone outside the table above.

Family health history

Family history is stored only on your device. We never save it to our servers or our database. When you use a feature that takes it into account — AI chat or a meal plan — a summary of the relevant risks is included in that single request to the AI provider, under the same terms as everything else in this section, and is not retained afterwards. If you delete the app, your family history goes with it.

Recipe photographs

A photograph you attach to a recipe you create is stored so it can be shown with that recipe, and is accessible to anyone who has its link. Please do not attach anything to a recipe that you would not want seen. This does not apply to plate photos or nutrition labels you scan, which are never stored.

Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted at the database level so one account cannot read another's, and the records that decide what your subscription unlocks can only be written by our server, never by an app.

Subscriptions and payments

Payment is taken by Apple or by Google Play, under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your card number, bank details or billing address.

To know which subscription you hold we use RevenueCat. When you sign in we give RevenueCat a pseudonymous identifier for your account — not your name and not your email address — and it validates your store receipt and tells our server which tier you are entitled to. RevenueCat receives no health data of any kind.

We store, against your account: your tier, the product you bought, when it expires and whether it is active. We keep that record after a subscription ends, so your purchase history stays accurate and an existing account is never wrongly locked out.

How long we keep it

  • Account and health data — until you delete your account.
  • Photographs sent for analysis — not retained by us at all.
  • Family history — on your device only, for as long as the app is installed.
  • Purchase records — kept after a subscription ends, and for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict how it is used, receive a copy in a portable format, and complain to a data protection authority. Under India's DPDP Act 2023 you may also nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf.

Most of this you can do in the app directly. For anything else, email support@heavit.com and we will respond within 30 days. We do not charge for this and we will not ask you to justify the request.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and the data associated with it from inside the app, or at heavit.com/delete-account. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Records we are legally required to keep — a purchase record, for instance — are retained for the period the law requires and nothing more.

Children

HeaVit is not intended for children under 13, or under 18 in jurisdictions that require parental consent for processing a child's data. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, email support@heavit.com and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when our processing changes, and will post the new effective date at the top. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
